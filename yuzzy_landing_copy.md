### Wording note (important):

I deliberately avoided "we secure/guarantee X membership" or "we get you a peer review invite" language here. Two reasons:

1. It's more accurate. Real fellowships, peer review invitations, and senior memberships are granted by the organizations themselves based on merit, not sold by a third party. "We help you prepare a strong application" or "we help you connect with opportunities" is honest about what the service actually does.
2. It's safer for him. USCIS adjudicators are increasingly trained to spot manufactured credentials. A landing page that says "we secure IEEE Fellow status" is a much bigger liability, both reputationally and possibly legally, than one that says "we help you build a competitive application." If a client's credential ever gets questioned in an RFE, softer wording holds up better.

If Yuzzy pushes back and wants stronger "we get you X" language, flag it to him directly rather than just softening it further on your own. It's his business and his call, but he should be making it with eyes open.
